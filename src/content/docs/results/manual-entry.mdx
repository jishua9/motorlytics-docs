When timing isn't published on Natsoft — club days, sprints, or events with paper results — you can enter the classification and lap times directly. Manual entry uses the same review table as [importing](/results/importing-results/), just started empty.

## Entering results manually

1. Open the session at **Events → (your event) → Sessions → (your session)** and select the **Results** tab.
2. On an empty Results tab, click **Input Manually**. (If results already exist, open the **Options** menu and choose **Edit Results**.)
3. A table titled **Add Results Manually** opens. Each row is one driver.
4. Fill in the columns for each entry:
   - **Pos** — finishing position (1–999)
   - **#** — race number
   - **Driver** — driver name
   - **Team** — team / competitor name
   - **Vehicle** — car make/model
   - **Laps** — laps completed (0–999)
   - **Time** — total time, format `0:00.000`
   - **Gap** — gap to leader, format `+0.000`
   - **Best Lap** — fastest lap time
   - **Status** — Finished, DNF, DNS or DSQ
5. Click **Add Row** for each additional driver. Use **Clear All** to start over (you'll be asked to confirm).
6. Click **Save Results** when the classification is complete.

Some columns (Team, Vehicle, Laps, Time, Gap, Best Lap) only render when relevant to the session type, so you may see a slightly shorter table for qualifying sessions.

## Entering lap times manually

Lap times are entered per driver, lap by lap, in a dedicated editor.

1. On the **Lap Times** area of the Results tab, open the manual lap-times editor. The modal is titled **Add Lap Times**.
2. Click **Add Another Driver**, enter the driver's **name** and **number**, then click **Add Driver**. Your own entry is tagged **You**.
3. For each lap, type the time across three inputs: **M** (minutes), **SS** (seconds) and **000** (thousandths).
4. Click **Add Lap** to add more laps for that driver.
5. As you type, a live **delta** column shows the change versus the previous lap — green for faster, red for slower — and the driver's best lap is starred.
6. Click **Save Lap Times** to save. When editing existing data the title reads **Edit Lap Times** and the button reads **Save Changes**.

## Editing later

Everything entered manually can be reopened and changed. Use **Options → Edit Results** for the classification, or reopen the lap-times editor to adjust individual laps. Saved manual data behaves exactly like imported data in the [lap-time and position charts](/results/lap-time-charts/).

## Tier availability

Manual entry of results and lap times is available on **all tiers** (Amateur, Pro and Team).
