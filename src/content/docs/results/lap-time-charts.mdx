Once a session has results, Motorlytics draws two charts on the **Results** tab: a lap-time comparison and, for races, a position chart. Both make your own line stand out and let you focus on the drivers you care about.

## Lap Times chart

Open the **Lap Times** area of the Results tab. Under the heading **Lap Times Comparison** you'll see a line chart:

- **X axis** is lap number (**Lap 1**, **Lap 2**, …).
- **Y axis** is lap time, shown in `M:SS.mmm`.
- **Your line** is drawn in bold red so it's easy to track.
- **Other drivers** use a rotating colour palette.
- A **dashed green reference line** marks the overall fastest lap of the session.

Click a driver in the **side legend** to show or hide their line — handy when the field is large and you only want to compare against a few rivals.

### Lap grid table

Below the chart is a sticky lap-by-lap table (columns **No.**, **Driver**, **Lap 1 … Lap N**). Each driver's **best lap is highlighted in green and bold**. Your own row is tinted and sorted to the top so you can find yourself instantly.

## Position Chart

For **Race** sessions, open the **Position Chart** area to see how the running order changed lap by lap:

- **X axis** is lap number.
- **Y axis** is position, **reversed so P1 is at the top**. The scale runs from P1 down to the number of drivers in the session.
- Hovering a point shows a tooltip with the position (`P{n}`) and driver name.
- The **legend is clickable** to toggle drivers on and off, the same as the lap-time chart.

If no lap chart has been imported yet, you'll see an empty state (**No Lap Chart Data**) with an **Import Lap Chart** button — see [Importing Results](/results/importing-results/).

## Best-lap highlighting at a glance

- **Dashed green line** on the Lap Times chart = the session's overall fastest lap.
- **Green/bold cell** in the lap grid = each driver's personal best lap.
- **Bold red line** = you.

## Weekend view

To compare across an entire event rather than a single session, the [Pit Wall](/pit-wall/overview/) analytics show a weekend position chart and per-session lap-time views. These advanced analytics are Pro/Team features.
