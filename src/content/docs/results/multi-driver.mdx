When several drivers share a car — or a race number doesn't match what's in the timing sheet — Motorlytics may highlight the wrong entry as "you". The Results tab lets you override which driver is treated as yours so the charts and highlighting follow the correct line.

## How your driver is chosen

By default, Motorlytics highlights the results row whose race number matches your **race number** for that event, set on your **Event Participation** when you join the event. Leading zeros are ignored, so `076` matches `76`.

At the top of the Results tab, a **Driver Identification Banner** shows who is currently selected, e.g. `Driver: Jane Smith #76 (P4)`. If no match is found, the banner tells you your number wasn't found in the results.

## Overriding the driver

1. Open the session's **Results** tab.
2. In the **Driver Identification Banner**, click **Not you?** (or **Select driver** if no match was found).
3. A popover titled **Select your driver from results** lists every entry in the classification.
4. Choose the correct driver. To go back to your event race number, choose **Use default (#X)**.

The override immediately re-highlights the correct row in the results table, the matching line in the [lap-time and position charts](/results/lap-time-charts/), and the banner text.

> **Scope:** This override is a view-only preference for the current session — it changes what's highlighted for you and is not saved back to the results or shared with other users.

## Career stats and shared cars

Separately from the on-screen override, Motorlytics records which result entry belongs to you when a session is linked, matching on your race number (again ignoring leading zeros). This is what populates your profile and career statistics. If two drivers share a number across sessions, make sure each driver's **race number** on their Event Participation is correct so their results are attributed properly.

## Tier availability

Driver selection and the override are available on **all tiers** (Amateur, Pro and Team).
