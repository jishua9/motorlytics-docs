Motorlytics imports official timing data by pasting it from the Natsoft results portal. There is no file upload step. You copy the text straight from the Natsoft page and paste it into Motorlytics, which parses the columns automatically.

> **Note on formats:** Some marketing copy refers to "CSV and Natsoft" imports. In practice, results are imported by copy-pasting Natsoft's text output; the parser handles the column layout for you. If your timing provider is not Natsoft, use [Manual Entry](/results/manual-entry/) instead.

## Where to import

Open the session you want results for at **Events → (your event) → Sessions → (your session)** and select the **Results** tab. The tab has three import areas, shown as sub-tabs/buttons:

- **Results**: the finishing classification (positions, drivers, times).
- **Position Chart** (import button labelled **Lap Chart**): lap-by-lap running order.
- **Lap Times**: every driver's individual lap times.

You must import **Results** first. The Position Chart and Lap Times imports link back to the classification, so they are unavailable until results exist.

## Importing the classification

1. In a browser, open the Natsoft results page for your session at `http://racing.natsoft.com.au/results/#2`.
2. Select all the text on the page (Ctrl+A) and copy it (Ctrl+C).
3. In Motorlytics, on the **Results** tab, choose to import. A modal titled **Import Results from Natsoft** appears with a monospace text box (placeholder *"Paste the copied Natsoft results here..."*).
4. Paste the copied text and click **Parse Results →**.
5. Review the parsed rows in the **Edit & Confirm Results** table. Fix anything that looks wrong (see below), then click **Save Results**.

### What the parser reads

The parser auto-detects the session type (qualifying vs race) and the column layout from the pasted header. It supports both the SA-style layout (`Pos Car Competitor/Team`) and the VIC-style layout (`Pos Car Driver`), so the driver and team columns are mapped correctly either way. It captures:

- **Position** and **car / race number**
- **Driver** and **Team / Competitor**
- **Vehicle**
- **Lap count, total time, gap and best lap** where present
- **Status**: finishers plus DNF, DNS and DSQ

Lap times are read in `M:SS.mmmm` format (four decimal places).

## Importing the position (lap) chart

The Position Chart shows the running order on each lap and is only available for **Race** sessions.

1. Copy the lap chart text from Natsoft for the same session.
2. On the **Position Chart** area of the Results tab, click **Import Lap Chart**.
3. Paste the text and confirm. Each lap's running order is mapped to the drivers already in your classification. If a chart is already present, the button reads **Re-import**.

## Importing lap times

1. Copy the individual/sector-and-lap-times text from Natsoft.
2. On the **Lap Times** area, click **Import Lap Times**.
3. Paste and confirm. Both the "Individual Lap Times" and "Sector and Lap Times" Natsoft layouts are supported.

## Editing imported data afterwards

Imported results are fully editable. Open the **Options** menu on the Results tab and choose **Edit Results** to reopen the same review table. You can change any cell (position, race number, driver, team, vehicle, laps, time, gap, best lap and status), add rows, or clear all rows, then **Save Results**. Lap times can likewise be corrected via the manual lap-times editor described in [Manual Entry](/results/manual-entry/).

## Availability

Results import, lap charts, and lap times are available with your Motorlytics subscription. Advanced weekend analytics that build on this data live in the [Pit Wall](/pit-wall/overview/) and are also included with your subscription.
