Motorlytics enforces per-tier limits on a few key resources. This page lists those limits and explains what happens when you hit them.

## Limits by tier

| Resource | Amateur | Pro | Team |
|----------|:-------:|:---:|:----:|
| Cars | 2 | 3 | Unlimited |
| Custom Metrics | 5 | 25 | 50 |
| Custom Setup Templates | 1 | 5 | 20 |

**Cars** — The count includes all active (non-archived) cars owned by your account. Team members on the Team tier share a single pool of team-owned cars; those do not count against your personal car total.

**Custom Metrics** — Metrics you create yourself under **Setup Sheets → Metrics**. The predefined metrics library is not counted against this limit.

**Custom Setup Templates** — Templates you create yourself under **Setup Sheets → Templates**. Predefined (system) templates are not counted.

## What happens when you reach a limit

### Cars

When you have reached your car limit and try to add another car, the **Add Car** form displays a banner:

> You've reached your car limit (X/X). Upgrade to Team for unlimited cars.

The **Save** button remains disabled until you either upgrade or archive an existing car.

### Custom Metrics

When you have reached your custom metric limit and try to create a new metric, the server returns an error:

> Custom metric limit reached (X). Upgrade to create more.

The metric is not saved. You must either upgrade or delete an existing custom metric before creating a new one.

### Custom Setup Templates

When you have reached your custom template limit and try to create a new template, the server returns an error:

> Custom template limit reached (X). Upgrade to create more.

The template is not saved. You must either upgrade or delete an existing custom template.

## Checking your current usage

Go to **Settings → Subscription**. The current plan card shows your tier. Your usage relative to limits is also surfaced inline when you open the **Add Car** form — it displays your current count versus the tier cap.

## Upgrading to increase limits

See [Upgrading Your Plan](/subscriptions/upgrading/) for the steps to move from Amateur to Pro, or contact support to arrange a Team subscription.
