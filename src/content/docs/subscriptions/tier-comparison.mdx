Motorlytics has three subscription tiers. New accounts start on a **14-day free trial** of the Pro plan — no charge on sign-up day.

## What each tier includes

### Amateur

The Amateur tier is a free, reduced-capability plan intended for casual or infrequent competitors. Core features — event tracking, session notes, setup sheets using predefined metrics, and basic lap time recording — are available. Data logger import and telemetry analysis are not included.

### Pro

Pro is the primary tier for active competitors. It adds data logger import (MoTeC CSV), full telemetry analysis (charts, lap delta, corner analysis, G-force, session health), and significantly higher limits for custom metrics and setup templates. Cost tracking and prior-part-KM tracking are also unlocked.

All new accounts start on a 14-day Pro trial. After the trial, a subscription is required to keep Pro features.

### Team

Team includes everything in Pro, plus multi-member team management: shared cars, shared custom metrics, and shared setup templates visible to all team members. The custom metric and template pools are team-level, separate from each individual member's personal limits.

Team tier is not available via self-serve checkout at this time — contact support to arrange a Team subscription.

## Pricing

Pricing is shown live during signup and on the **Settings → Subscription** page. Pro is available on monthly or yearly billing; yearly billing offers a discount over the monthly rate.
