Motorlytics costs **$15/month AUD**. All new accounts start on a **14-day free trial**. No charge on sign-up day. Annual billing is also available (price shown at checkout).

## Starting the free trial

The **Plan & Payment** step of the signup wizard lets you choose Monthly or Yearly billing and enter your card details via Stripe's secure payment form. Your card is not charged on the day you sign up. The 14-day trial runs first, and billing begins automatically when the trial ends unless you cancel.

A "14-day free trial" callout is displayed on the payment screen confirming no charge today.

## Managing your subscription after signup

All subscription actions are on the **Settings → Subscription** tab.

### Subscribing after a trial expires

If your trial has ended and you have not yet subscribed, a red banner appears at the top of every page. Click **Subscribe** in that banner, or go to **Settings → Subscription** and click **Subscribe Now**. You will be taken to a Stripe Checkout page to enter payment details and choose Monthly or Yearly billing.

### Switching billing interval (Monthly to Yearly or back)

On the **Settings → Subscription** tab, find the **Billing cycle** row under your current plan. Click the **Change** link next to the interval, or use the **Change Plan** button in the **Manage Subscription** section. The **Switch Plan** dialog lets you switch between Monthly and Yearly. Changes are prorated automatically by Stripe.

### Cancelling your subscription

On the **Settings → Subscription** tab, click **Cancel Subscription** in the **Manage Subscription** section. A confirmation prompt appears: "Are you sure you want to cancel your subscription? You'll keep access until the end of your billing period." Confirm to schedule cancellation. Your plan remains active until the billing period ends. A yellow **Subscription Ending** banner shows the exact end date.

### Resuming a pending cancellation

If you have cancelled but your billing period has not yet ended, click **Resume Subscription** in the yellow **Subscription Ending** banner on the **Settings → Subscription** tab. This cancels the scheduled cancellation and your subscription continues normally.

### Resubscribing after cancellation

After your subscription ends, the **Settings → Subscription** tab shows a **Resubscribe** section. Select Monthly or Yearly and click **Subscribe**. Because you have an existing Stripe customer record, you are not given another trial. Billing begins immediately on resubscription.

## Payment methods

The **Payment Methods** section on **Settings → Subscription** shows all cards on file. Click **Add Card** to add a new card. Set a card as default by clicking the tick icon next to it. Remove a non-default card with the trash icon.
