Motorlytics has one subscription plan at **$15/month AUD**, with a **14-day free trial** on sign-up. Annual billing is also available. Every subscriber gets all current features, including data-logger telemetry import and analysis, corner analysis, G-force, lap-delta comparison, cost tracking, prior-part-KM tracking, setup sheets, race results, and the Pit Wall.

## What is included

All current features are included with your subscription. There are no locked or gated features based on a plan tier.

A small number of resource limits are enforced:

| Resource | Limit |
|----------|:-----:|
| Cars | Up to 3 |
| Custom Metrics | Up to 25 |
| Custom Setup Templates | Up to 5 |

When you reach a limit you will be prompted to remove or replace an existing item. There is no higher tier to upgrade to for more capacity.

## Team features (coming soon)

Team features (shared cars, shared custom metrics and templates, Owner/Admin/Member roles) are on the roadmap and are not yet available. They will not require a separate purchase when released.

## Enforced limits

Custom metric and custom template counts are tracked per user. Motorlytics checks these counts before allowing you to create a new custom metric or template and will prompt you to remove an existing one if you have reached the limit.

For a full breakdown of what happens at each limit, see the Plan Limits page.
