Motorlytics has three subscription tiers — Amateur, Pro, and Team — each aimed at a different stage of your motorsport journey. All three tiers include the core event, session, and car-management features. The tiers differ in how many cars and customisations you can create, whether data-logger telemetry is available, and whether team sharing is unlocked.

## Tiers at a Glance

| Feature | Amateur | Pro | Team |
|---------|:-------:|:---:|:----:|
| Cars | 2 | 3 | Unlimited |
| Custom Metrics | 5 | 25 | 50 |
| Custom Templates | 1 | 5 | 20 |
| Data Logger Import | — | Yes | Yes |
| Telemetry Storage | — | Yes | Yes |
| Advanced Analytics | — | Yes | Yes |
| Cost Tracking | — | Yes | Yes |
| Prior Part KM | — | Yes | Yes |
| Team Features | — | — | Yes |
| Shared Cars | — | — | Yes |

### Amateur

The free tier. You can manage up to two cars, record sessions and setup data, import race results, and use the Pit Wall. Data-logger import and telemetry analysis are not included. Custom setup sheets are capped at one template and five custom metrics.

### Pro

Aimed at club and state-series competitors who want full data analysis. The car limit rises to three, telemetry import and storage are enabled, and you get advanced analytics including corner analysis, G-force visualisation, and lap-delta comparison. Cost tracking and prior part KM recording are also unlocked. Custom templates expand to five, and custom metrics to 25.

### Team

Everything in Pro, plus team management. You can create a team, invite members (Owner / Admin / Member roles), and share cars, setup templates, and custom metrics across the team. Shared custom metrics expand to 50 and custom templates to 20.

## Enforced Limits

The custom-metric and custom-template counts are tracked per user (and per team on the Team tier). Motorlytics checks these counts before allowing you to create a new custom metric or template and will prompt you to upgrade if you have reached your tier's limit.
