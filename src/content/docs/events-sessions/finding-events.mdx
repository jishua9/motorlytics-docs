The Events page at `/events` is your hub for discovering upcoming motorsport events and keeping track of the ones you have entered.

## My Events tab

The **My Events** tab shows every event you have already joined, sorted by date.

1. Navigate to **Events** in the sidebar.
2. The **My Events** tab is open by default. Your upcoming joined events appear in a table showing event name, venue, dates, and source (manual or Motorsport Australia).
3. Use the **search box** to filter by event name or track name.
4. Use the **date range selector** to narrow results to *All Upcoming*, *This Month*, or *Next 3 Months*.
5. Click any row to open the event detail page, where you can view your participation details and go to the Pit Wall.

## Find Events tab

The **Find Events** tab pulls live events from the Motorsport Australia calendar and presents them alongside a mini calendar.

1. Click the **Find Events** tab. Events for the next three months load automatically, pre-filtered to your profile's home state if you have one set.
2. The left column shows a **calendar view**. Click a date to scroll the event list to that day. Click an event dot to highlight the event in the list.
3. The right column is the **event list**. Use the filter controls to narrow results:
   - **State** — filter by Australian state or territory.
   - **Discipline** — filter by racing discipline (circuit, motorkhana, hill climb, etc.).
   - **Month** — jump to a specific calendar month.
   - **This Weekend / Next Weekend** — quick-filter buttons to show only the relevant two-day window.
4. Click **Search** to apply the filters and refresh the list.
5. Click any event in the list to open the **join form** for that event (see [Creating Your First Event](/getting-started/creating-your-first-event/) for join form details). Already-joined events show a green tick and cannot be clicked again.

## Joining an event

1. In **Find Events**, click an event to expand the join form below it.
2. Select the **Car** you are entering.
3. Enter your **Race Number** and **Category** (auto-filled from your car profile if available).
4. Click **Join Event**. The event is added to your **My Events** tab immediately.

## Participant list

From any event you have joined:

1. Go to **Events → My Events** and click the event row.
2. The event detail page shows a **Participants** card listing every driver who has joined, with their car, race number, and category.
