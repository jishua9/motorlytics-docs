The **Notes** tab and the **Complete Session** flow together capture the qualitative side of a session: your observations, car behaviour, follow-up tasks, and how the session went overall.

## Session Notes tab

Open a session and click the **Notes** tab. The tab is divided into three sections.

### Session Notes

A free-text area for anything you want to remember about the session, such as tyre behaviour, track conditions, setup changes you made during the session, or driver coaching feedback. Notes auto-save as you type (debounced, about 0.8 seconds after you stop).

### Car Feedback

A set of five sliders that capture a quick read on how the car felt:

| Slider | Options |
|--------|---------|
| Balance | Understeer · Neutral · Oversteer |
| Stability | Unstable · Neutral · Stable |
| Grip | Low · Medium · High |
| Braking | Weak · Balanced · Strong |
| Acceleration | Wheelspin · Neutral · Traction |

Click the desired option on each slider. Changes save automatically alongside your notes.

### Car Action Items

A checklist for follow-up tasks identified during the session, for example *check front damper settings* or *replace rear brake pads*. Action items are linked to the car, not just the session, so they appear in the car's maintenance view as well.

1. Click **Add action item** and type the task description.
2. Tick the checkbox when the task is done.
3. Items persist against the car until you delete them.

## Completing a session

After a session ends, use **Complete Session** to log the final lap count and best time. This also adds the distance driven to your car's odometer.

1. From the session detail page, click the **Complete Session** button in the session header (the button is labelled *Edit Completion* if you have already completed it).
2. Fill in the **Complete Session** dialog:
   - **Laps Completed** *(required)*: the total number of laps you completed. If you have imported race results, this field is pre-filled from the results data.
   - **Best Lap** *(optional)*: enter your fastest lap in M:SS.mmm format using the three split fields (minutes, seconds, milliseconds). Pre-filled from results if available.
   - **Rating** *(optional)*: click a star to rate the session 1 to 5. This overrides or sets the rating from the Setup tab.
   - **Notes** *(optional)*: a short summary note saved to the session.
   - **Continue to add detailed lap times**: tick this checkbox to jump directly to the **Results** tab after saving, where you can enter individual lap times manually.
3. Click **Complete Session** (or **Update Completion** in edit mode).
4. A confirmation shows the kilometres added to your car, for example *Added 88.96 km to Formula Ford #76*.

> **When results are imported:** If you have already imported Natsoft results for this session, the lap count and best lap are pre-filled and the car's odometer has already been updated from the results data. Completing the session in this case records your rating and notes without double-counting kilometres.
