Service records give you a permanent maintenance log tied to your car's odometer. Each service can install new tracked parts, retire old ones, and close outstanding action items in one step.

## Logging a service

1. Go to the car's **Maintenance** tab and locate the **Services** panel.
2. Click **Add Service**.
3. Enter a **Service Title** (e.g., Oil Change, Brake Service, Gearbox Rebuild).
4. Choose a **Category**: Engine, Transmission, Suspension, Brakes, Tyres, Fluids, Electrical, Chassis, or Other.
5. Set the **Date** using the date picker.
6. Confirm or edit the **KM at Service** field — it pre-fills with the car's current Racing KM.
7. Add a **Description** of what was done (optional but recommended).
8. Fill in **Cost** and **Service Provider** if applicable. Cost tracking requires a Pro or Team subscription.
9. Add **Additional Notes** for invoice numbers, warranty info, or follow-up reminders.
10. Click **Save Service**.

## Linking parts to a service

### Installing new parts

In the **Parts Installed** section of the Add Service dialog, click **Add Part** to add one or more parts being installed during this service. For each part you can:

- Set a name and category.
- Enable **Track this part** to record a **Lifespan (km)** and optional expiry date. Tracked parts appear in the car's parts lifecycle list with their KM counter starting from this service.
- Leave **Track this part** unchecked to record the part in the service log without lifecycle tracking (useful for consumables you don't need to monitor).

### Replacing existing parts

In the **Parts to Replace** section, check any active tracked parts that are being swapped out during this service. Replacing a part archives the old record and starts a fresh lifecycle entry.

## Closing action items via a service

If the car has pending action items, a **Close Related Action Item?** section appears at the bottom of the form. Check any action items that this service resolves. They will be marked complete with a reference back to this service record, so you can always see which service addressed a given issue.

## Viewing past services

All logged services are listed in the Services panel on the **Maintenance** tab, sorted by date descending. Click any service row to see the full record, including linked parts and action items. Use the three-dot menu on a service row to **Edit** or **Delete** the record.

## Cost tracking

The **Cost** field on each service (and on individual parts when adding them) is aggregated in the **Car Costs** section available to Pro and Team subscribers. This lets you see total spend per category over time.

**Cost Tracking requires a Pro or Team subscription.**
