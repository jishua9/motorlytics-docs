Your car profile is the hub for everything related to a specific vehicle — specs, race history, setup sheets, parts, tyres, and services are all accessed from here.

## Adding a car

1. From the dashboard, select **Add Car** (or navigate to `/cars/new`).
2. Fill in **Make** and **Model** (required), then optionally add **Year**, **Colour**, **Category**, and **Race Number**.
3. Under **Technical Specs**, enter **Engine**, **Capacity**, **Chassis Number**, and **Starting KM** (the odometer reading when you began tracking this car in Motorlytics).
4. Under **Racing Details**, add **Transponder** and any other racing-specific fields.
5. Under **Registration**, record **Logbook**, **Plate**, and **COD** numbers if needed.
6. Click **Add Car**. You will be taken directly to the new car's profile page.

Amateur accounts can hold up to 2 cars. Pro and Team accounts have no limit.

## Viewing and editing a car profile

Navigate to a car by clicking it on the dashboard or going to `/cars/[id]`. The car page has four tabs:

| Tab | What's here |
|-----|-------------|
| **Overview** | Car Details card, Recent Activity, Notes, Action Items |
| **Setup** | Setup sheet templates assigned to this car |
| **History** | Race history table |
| **Maintenance** | Parts, Tyre Sets, and Service records |

To edit car details, open the **Car Details** card on the Overview tab, click the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of the card, and select **Edit Car**.

## Personal vs team cars

A car belongs to either a personal account or a team:

- **Personal car** — only you can see and manage it.
- **Team car** — visible to all team members. Team cars show the team name as a badge on the car profile. Only Team tier accounts can create team cars.

## Car notes

The **Notes** card on the Overview tab holds free-text notes about the car (VIN, oil spec, radio frequency, tyre pressures, etc.).

1. Click **Edit** in the Notes card header.
2. Type or paste your notes into the text area.
3. Click **Save**.

## Action items

The **Action Items** card tracks to-dos for the car. Action items can also be created automatically from session notes.

1. Type a task description into the **Add an action item…** field.
2. Set the priority: **Urgent**, **Before Next Event**, or **When Convenient**.
3. Click the **+** button to add it.

To mark an item done, click the checkbox beside it or open its menu and choose **Mark Complete**. Completed items remain visible in a collapsed list at the bottom of the card and can be reopened or deleted. Action items can also be closed via a service record — see [Service Records](/cars/service-records/).
