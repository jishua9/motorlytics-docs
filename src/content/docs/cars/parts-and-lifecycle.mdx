Motorlytics tracks every part on your race car against the car's racing odometer, so you always know what percentage of a part's life has been used and how many kilometres remain before replacement is due.

## Adding a part

1. Go to the car's **Maintenance** tab and locate the **Parts** panel.
2. Click **Add Part**.
3. Enter the **Part Name** (e.g., Engine Oil, Brake Pads, Gearbox) and choose a **Category** (Engine, Transmission, Suspension, Brakes, Tyres, Fluids, Electrical, Chassis, or Other).
4. Set the **Installation Date** using the date picker.
5. Enter **Lifespan (KM)**: the total kilometres the part is expected to last.
6. Optionally set an **Expires On** date for time-sensitive parts such as brake fluid or coolant.
7. Expand **More Details** to add **Brand**, **Part Number**, **Cost ($)**, and **Notes**.
8. Click **Add Part**.

The part is installed at the car's current Racing KM, which becomes its **Installation KM**.

## How lifecycle is calculated

Once a part is installed, Motorlytics calculates its lifecycle as:

```
Current Life KM  = Car Racing KM − Installation KM
Remaining Life KM = Lifespan KM − Current Life KM
% Used           = Current Life KM ÷ Lifespan KM × 100
```

If you also set an **Expires On** date, Motorlytics calculates a time-based percentage in parallel and uses whichever is worse (higher percentage) as the overall status.

### Status thresholds

| % Used | Status |
|--------|--------|
| 0 to 69% | Healthy |
| 70 to 89% | Monitor |
| 90 to 99% | Replace Soon |
| 100%+ | Overdue |

These thresholds are fixed. Adjust your **Lifespan (KM)** value to tune when warnings appear for your specific parts.

## Installing a used part (Prior Part KM)

If you are fitting a part that already has some kilometres on it, enable the **Used Part** toggle in the Add Part dialog. Enter the **Prior KM on Part** (how many kilometres are already on the part). Motorlytics will back-calculate the effective Installation KM so the lifecycle counter starts at the correct percentage.

**Prior Part KM is included with your Motorlytics subscription.**

## Replacing a part

When a part reaches end of life, open its row in the Parts panel (three-dot menu or click the part) and choose **Replace Part**. This:

1. Archives the old part and records it in the replacement chain.
2. Creates a new part record with reset lifecycle, using the car's current Racing KM as the new Installation KM.
3. Preserves the same Part Name and lifespan by default. Edit them if the replacement spec differs.

If fitting a used replacement, enable **Used Replacement** and enter **Prior KM on Replacement** to start the new part's lifecycle at the right point.

### Replacement chains

Each replaced part keeps a reference to its predecessor. This creates a full history of a part slot (e.g., every set of brake pads ever fitted), useful for auditing and warranty tracking.

## Removing a part

If a part is removed without being replaced (e.g., a weight reduction or category change), open the part's menu and choose **Remove Part**. The part is marked inactive and no longer appears in the active parts list.
