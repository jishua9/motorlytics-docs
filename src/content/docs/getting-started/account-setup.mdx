This page walks through creating a new Motorlytics account and keeping your profile up to date after you sign in.

## Signing up

Go to [app.motorlytics.com.au/signup](https://app.motorlytics.com.au/signup). The signup wizard has three steps shown in the progress bar at the top: **Account**, **Car Details**, and **Plan & Payment**.

### Step 1 — Account

Fill in the following fields:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Profile Photo | No | You can add or change this at any time after signup. |
| Full Name | Yes | Minimum 2 characters. |
| Email | Yes | Must be unique. The form checks availability in real time. |
| Password | Yes | Minimum 8 characters. |
| Confirm Password | Yes | Must match the password field. |

**Racing Licence (optional)** — expand this section to record your licence before your first event:

- **Licence Type** — select from grouped categories (Motorsport Australia, State, International, etc.)
- **Licence Number** — your issued licence number
- **Expiry Date** — DD/MM/YYYY format

Click **Continue** to proceed to the Car Details step.

### Step 2 — Car Details

Add your first race car. See [Adding Your First Car](/getting-started/adding-your-first-car/) for a full field breakdown.

### Step 3 — Plan & Payment

Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** billing, then enter your payment details via Stripe. Your subscription starts after a **14-day free trial** — you will not be charged on signup day.

If you want to explore the app before committing, click **Skip for now — I'll add payment later** at the bottom of this step.

## Updating your profile

After signup, go to **Settings** (accessible from the sidebar or account menu) to edit your profile.

### Profile Information

- **Profile Photo** — click **Update Profile Photo** to upload a new image.
- **Name** — your display name across the app.
- **Email** — read-only after account creation. Verified accounts show a **Verified** badge.

### Club Details

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Club Name | e.g. ARDC, MSCA |
| Club Number | Your membership number |
| Club Expiry | Membership expiry date (DD/MM/YYYY) |

### Licence Details

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Licence Type | Select from grouped categories |
| Licence Number | Your issued licence number |
| Licence Expiry | DD/MM/YYYY format |

Click **Save Changes** to apply updates.

## Password reset

If you forget your password, use the **Forgot password?** link on the login page at `/login`. You will receive a reset email; follow the link to set a new password at `/reset-password`.
