When you first sign in, a small **Setup guide** button sits in the bottom right corner of every page. It shows how many setup jobs you have done, for example `2/5`, with a thin progress bar. Click it to open the checklist, and click it again to close it.

The guide is there to get a new account ready for its first race weekend. Once all five jobs are done it says "You're set up" and goes away for good.

## The five steps

Steps tick themselves off from your real data. Nothing is marked done by hand, so a step unticks again if you delete what ticked it.

| Step | Ticks when | Where to do it |
|---|---|---|
| Join an event | You have entered any event | **Events** in the sidebar |
| Create your setup sheet | You have a setup sheet you made yourself and still have | Car page, **Setup** tab |
| Save your baseline setup | One of your presets is set as a car's default | Car page, **Setup** tab |
| Add tyres | You have a tyre set on one of your cars | Car page, **Maintenance** tab |
| Track a part | You have added a part yourself | Car page, **Maintenance** tab |

Clicking a step takes you to the right page and closes the list.

A few details worth knowing:

- **Setup sheets:** the sheets included with Motorlytics do not count, because they are not yours. Building one from scratch and duplicating an included one both do. See [Custom Templates](/setup-sheets/custom-templates/).
- **Baseline setup:** this is a preset with the star set, which fills in the first session of each weekend. Even your stock sheet values are worth saving as one. See [Presets and Baselines](/setup-sheets/presets-and-baselines/).
- **Tyres:** a retired set still counts, since it is still tyres you have recorded. A deleted set does not. See [Tyres](/cars/tyres/).
- **Parts:** the Engine, Engine Oil and Gearbox that come with every new car do not count, only a part you added. See [Parts and Lifecycle](/cars/parts-and-lifecycle/).

Steps tick as soon as you save, so you do not need to change page to see the checklist update.

## Minimising and turning it off

**Minimise**, at the foot of the list, closes the list and leaves the button in the corner. Your choice is remembered in that browser, so the list stays closed until you open it again. The button itself stays until all five jobs are done.

To remove the guide completely, go to **Settings**, then **Preferences**, and turn off **Show setup guide**. It saves as soon as you flip it. Turn it back on there at any time. Once you have finished all five jobs the switch is greyed out, because the guide is already done with.

Your progress is stored on your account, so it looks the same on any computer you sign in on. Whether the list is open or closed is remembered per browser.

## Page tips

The first time you open one of the busier screens, a short card appears in the same corner explaining what the screen is for, with a link to the matching page here in the docs.

| Tip | Appears on |
|---|---|
| Pit Wall in 30 seconds | The Pit Wall for an event |
| Your weekend review | An event page after the event has finished |
| Reading the analysis | The Analytics tab, on the Pit Wall or the weekend review |
| Your car page | Any car page |

Only one tip shows at a time, and a tip waits while the setup guide list is open. Click **Got it** to dismiss a tip, or **Read the docs** to open the related page. A tip is only marked as seen once you do one of those, so leaving the page does not use it up.

To see the tips again, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Replay page tips**.
