Every session, setup sheet, and part lifecycle in Motorlytics is tied to a car. This page explains how to create your first car and what each field means.

## During signup

The quickest way to add a car is in the **Car Details** step of the signup wizard (step 2 of 3). Fill in the fields and click **Continue** — you can always edit or add more detail later.

Fields available during signup:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Make | Yes | e.g. Toyota, Van Diemen |
| Model | Yes | e.g. 86, RF99 |
| Year | No | Four-digit year |
| Category | Yes | Select from predefined racing categories (Formula Ford, Excel, GT, Touring Car, and more) |
| Race Number | No | Auto-filled from the car on future events |
| Colour | No | |
| Car Photo | No | Upload an optional photo of your car |
| Starting KM | No | Set this to the odometer reading when you start tracking — used for part lifecycle calculations |

## Adding a car after signup

Go to **Cars** in the sidebar, then click **Add Car** (or navigate directly to `/cars/new`). The full create form has four sections:

### Car Details (required)

- **Make** and **Model** — the vehicle's make and model.
- **Year** — model year.
- **Colour** — optional.
- **Category** — select from predefined racing categories. This ties the car to the correct setup templates and class-based filtering.

### Technical Specs

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Engine | Engine code or description, e.g. 2ZZ-GE |
| Capacity | e.g. 1.8L |
| Chassis Number | Used for logbook and compliance identification |
| Starting KM | Odometer reading at time of first entry — affects part lifecycle tracking |

### Racing Details

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Race Number | Pre-fills when you join events |
| Transponder | Your transponder ID |

### Registration

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Logbook | Logbook number |
| Plate | Registration plate |
| COD | Certificate of Description number |

Click **Add Car** to save.

## Car limits by subscription tier

The number of cars you can add depends on your subscription:

- **Amateur** — limited number of personal cars
- **Pro** — higher limit; upgrade prompt appears when the limit is reached
- **Team** — unlimited cars; team cars are shared across all team members

When you reach your limit, the form shows an **Upgrade required** notice with a link to `/subscription`.

## Multiple cars and team cars

You can manage several cars from the **Cars** section. Each car has its own parts inventory, service history, odometer, tyre sets, and setup configurations.

If you are a member of a team, shared team cars appear alongside your personal cars throughout the app.

See [Managing Cars](/cars/managing-cars/) for full details on editing, deleting, and team car access.
