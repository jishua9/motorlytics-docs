Follow these four steps to go from a brand-new account to having your first session ready to review.

## Step 1: Create your account

Go to [app.motorlytics.com.au/signup](https://app.motorlytics.com.au/signup). The signup wizard walks you through three screens: **Account**, **Car Details**, and **Plan & Payment**.

You can start with a 14-day free trial. No charge on the day you sign up.

See [Account Setup](/getting-started/account-setup/) for a full field-by-field walkthrough.

## Step 2: Add your first car

The **Car Details** step of the signup wizard is the fastest way to add your first car. Fill in Make, Model, Category, and optionally a Race Number and Starting KM, then continue.

You can add more cars later from **Cars** in the sidebar, or manage existing ones from the same section.

See [Adding Your First Car](/getting-started/adding-your-first-car/) for details on every field and multi-car setup.

## Step 3: Create or join an event

Navigate to **Events** in the sidebar. Use the **Find Events** tab to browse Motorsport Australia calendar events and join one with your race number and category, or click **Add Event** and choose **Create Custom** to create a private track day or test session.

See [Creating Your First Event](/getting-started/creating-your-first-event/) for a step-by-step walkthrough of both paths.

## Step 4: Record a session

Open your event, then add a session (Practice, Qualifying, or Race). Each session is where you record your car setup, driver notes, and lap times for that run.

See [Session Types](/events-sessions/session-types/) for a breakdown of what each session type captures.
