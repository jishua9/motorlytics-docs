The Pit Wall is the live command centre for an event you have joined. It pulls your sessions, weather, results, and quick stats into a single view so you can stay focused on driving rather than navigating between screens.

## Opening the Pit Wall

The Pit Wall is available for any event you have joined (i.e. you have an active participation with a car assigned).

1. Navigate to **Events** in the sidebar and find the event.
2. Click **Pit Wall** in the event row, or open the event detail and click **Go to Pit Wall**.
3. The Pit Wall opens full-screen at `/events/[id]/pitwall`.

You can also reach it directly from the dashboard calendar card. Clicking an event you have joined takes you straight to the Pit Wall.

## Title Bar

Across the top of every Pit Wall screen, a persistent title bar shows:

- **Event name**, track layout, and your car's race number and category.
- **Per-day weather forecast** for the event weekend: date, condition icon, high temperature, and rain probability.
- **Countdown timer** counting down to the next session. The timer shows `HH:MM:SS` or `MM:SS` depending on remaining time. The bar changes colour to amber (imminent, within 5 minutes) or green (in-progress) and a progress bar appears once a session is live.

When no sessions have been added yet, the timer area shows "No upcoming sessions. Add a session to get started."

## Tabs

The Pit Wall is divided into four tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Dashboard** | Sessions schedule, quick weather summary, results, lap times, quick stats, and best-match setup |
| **Weather** | Full weather forecast, hourly timeline, track wind diagram, and animated rain radar |
| **Analytics** | Lap time analysis and setup correlation across the weekend |
| **Circuit** | Track map and circuit annotation tools |

A **Settings** dropdown at the far right of the tab bar gives access to **Edit Event** and **Leave Event**.

## Dashboard Layout

The Dashboard tab is arranged in three columns:

**Left column**
- **Sessions**: your session schedule with live status indicators and best-lap times. See [Session Management](/pit-wall/session-management/) for full detail.
- **Action Items**: tasks and notes raised against the car for this event.

**Centre column**
- **Weather**: a compact summary of current conditions with a session timeline overlay. Full weather lives on the Weather tab.
- A tabbed panel showing session results and lap times for the event.

**Right column**
- **Quick Stats**: delta comparisons across sessions anchored to a chosen baseline session (defaults to the most recent completed race).
- **Best Match**: the historical setup most closely matching current conditions at this circuit.
