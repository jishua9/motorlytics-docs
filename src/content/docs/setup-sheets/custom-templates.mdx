Custom templates let you build a setup sheet tailored to your car and driving style. You can add your own sections, choose which metrics appear in each section, and reorder everything with drag and drop. Custom templates are available on the **Pro** and **Team** subscription tiers.

:::note[Tier limits]
- **Pro** — up to 5 custom templates
- **Team** — up to 20 custom templates
- **Amateur** — predefined templates only; upgrade to create custom ones
:::

## Creating a new template

1. Navigate to **Cars** and open the car you want the template for.
2. Select the **Setup** tab.
3. In the **Templates** panel, click **Create**.
   - If you are on the Amateur tier, a lock icon appears instead — click it to see upgrade options.
4. Give the template a **name** (required, up to 100 characters) and choose a **category** (Basic, Formula, GT, Touring Car, Sports Car, or Custom).
5. Click **Create** to open the template editor at `/setup/configure`.

Alternatively, you can **duplicate** any existing template — predefined or custom — by clicking the duplicate icon on a template item in the list. Duplicating counts toward your custom template limit.

## Using the template editor

The editor at `/setup/configure` has two panels side-by-side:

- **Left panel** — the sections and metrics you have already added. Drag metrics up or down within a section to reorder them, or drag them to a different section.
- **Right panel (Metrics Sidebar)** — all available metrics, grouped by category. Drag a metric from the sidebar and drop it into any section on the left.

### Adding and renaming sections

1. In the editor, click **Add Section** to append a new section at the bottom.
2. Click a section heading to rename it (up to 100 characters). Optionally add a short description.
3. To make an entire section lay out in a four-corner grid (FL / FR / RL / RR), enable the **Corner-based section** toggle — see [Corner-Based Metrics](/setup-sheets/corner-based-metrics/).
4. Drag the section handle to reorder sections.
5. To delete a section, click the trash icon. Any metrics inside are removed from the template (they are not deleted globally).

### Adding metrics from the sidebar

1. In the Metrics Sidebar, browse or search for a metric by name or category.
2. Drag the metric card from the sidebar and drop it on a section. The metric appears at the bottom of that section.
3. You can also drag metrics between sections to reorganise.
4. To remove a metric from the template, drag it back to the sidebar or click the remove icon on the metric card.

### Maximum metrics per template

Each template has a **Max Metrics** limit (1–100, default 50). The editor shows a count in the header (e.g., "12 / 50"). When the limit is reached, you cannot add more metrics until you remove some.

### Saving changes

The editor does not auto-save. Click **Save Changes** (top right) when you are done. The button is disabled when there are no unsaved changes. A toast notification confirms success and summarises how many metrics were added, removed, or updated.

## Editing an existing custom template

1. Go to the car's **Setup** tab.
2. Find your custom template in the **Custom** section of the templates list.
3. Click the pencil/edit icon to open it in the template editor.

You cannot edit predefined templates. Duplicate them first to get an editable copy.

## Deleting a custom template

1. In the car's **Setup** tab, hover over the custom template.
2. Click the delete icon and confirm.
3. Deletion is permanent and decrements your custom template count.

:::caution
A template cannot be deleted while it is set as a car's default template. Assign a different default first.
:::
