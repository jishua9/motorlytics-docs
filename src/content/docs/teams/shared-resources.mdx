Shared resources are a **Team tier** feature. When a resource belongs to a team, every team member — regardless of role — can read and use it.

## Shared cars

A car registered under a team appears in the car list for all team members. Any member can:

- View the car's details, parts, and service records
- Record sessions using the team car
- Log tyre usage and odometer adjustments

Adding a car to the team rather than to your personal account means all drivers in a shared-drive or endurance situation work from the same vehicle record. The car's maintenance history, parts lifecycle, and odometer are a single source of truth for the whole team.

To create a car under the team, the Owner or Admin assigns the car to the team during car creation. See [Managing Cars](/cars/managing-cars/) for the full car setup process.

## Team custom metrics

Custom setup metrics created under the team are available to every member when filling out setup sheets. This keeps everyone using the same metric names and units — for example a team-standard tyre temperature probe position or a custom damper setting.

Team-level limits apply to all custom metrics owned by the team:

| Tier | Custom metrics |
|------|---------------|
| Team | 50 |

Owners and Admins can create, edit, and delete team metrics. Members can use them but cannot modify them.

See [Custom Metrics](/setup-sheets/custom-metrics/) for instructions on creating and editing metrics.

## Team setup templates

Setup sheet templates owned by the team appear in the template list for all members. Drivers use the same baseline setup document across every session, which makes comparing data between drivers on the same car straightforward.

Team-level template limits:

| Tier | Custom templates |
|------|-----------------|
| Team | 20 |

Owners and Admins manage team templates. Members can apply them to sessions and view setup history.

See [Custom Templates](/setup-sheets/custom-templates/) for the drag-and-drop template editor.

## How sharing works in practice

When a team member opens a shared car, metric, or template they see it alongside their personal resources — there is no separate "team view". The car list shows both personal and team cars. The template picker shows both personal and team templates. Resources are labelled with the team name so it is always clear which belong to the team.

Deleting a team resource removes it for every member. Personal resources are never affected.
