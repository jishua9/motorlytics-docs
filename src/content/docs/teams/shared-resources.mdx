:::note[Coming soon]
Shared resources (team cars, team custom metrics, and team setup templates) are on the roadmap and are not yet available. This page describes the planned behaviour when team features are released.
:::

## Shared cars

When team features are released, a car registered under a team will appear in the car list for all team members. Any member will be able to:

- View the car's details, parts, and service records
- Record sessions using the team car
- Log tyre usage and odometer adjustments

Adding a car to the team rather than to your personal account means all drivers in a shared-drive or endurance situation work from the same vehicle record. The car's maintenance history, parts lifecycle, and odometer will be a single source of truth for the whole team.

## Team custom metrics

Custom setup metrics created under the team will be available to every member when filling out setup sheets. This keeps everyone using the same metric names and units, for example a team-standard tyre temperature probe position or a custom damper setting.

Owners and Admins will be able to create, edit, and delete team metrics. Members will be able to use them but not modify them.

See [Custom Metrics](/setup-sheets/custom-metrics/) for instructions on creating and editing metrics.

## Team setup templates

Setup sheet templates owned by the team will appear in the template list for all members. Drivers can use the same baseline setup document across every session, which makes comparing data between drivers on the same car straightforward.

Owners and Admins will manage team templates. Members will be able to apply them to sessions and view setup history.

See [Custom Templates](/setup-sheets/custom-templates/) for the drag-and-drop template editor.

## How sharing will work in practice

When a team member opens a shared car, metric, or template they will see it alongside their personal resources. There will be no separate "team view". The car list will show both personal and team cars. The template picker will show both personal and team templates. Resources will be labelled with the team name so it is always clear which belong to the team.

Deleting a team resource will remove it for every member. Personal resources are never affected.
