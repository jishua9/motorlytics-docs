:::note[Coming soon]
Team member management is on the roadmap and is not yet available as a self-serve feature. This page documents the planned role model and how member management will work when the feature is released.
:::

## Team roles

Motorlytics plans three team roles that control what each person can do within the team.

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full control over the team. Can invite and remove members, change roles, manage team settings, add and remove team cars, and create or delete shared resources. There must always be at least one Owner. |
| **Admin** | Can invite members and manage shared resources (cars, metrics, templates). Cannot change the Owner's role or delete the team. |
| **Member** | Can access all shared team resources (cars, templates, and metrics) but cannot invite others or change team settings. |

## Current process for adding a driver

:::note[No self-serve invite UI yet]
To add a driver to your team, contact Motorlytics support with the driver's registered email address and the role you want to assign (Admin or Member). Support will link the account to your team directly.
:::

Once added, the driver will see your team listed under **Settings → Account → Team Memberships**. The driver must already have a Motorlytics account before they can be added.

## Changing a member's role

Owners can change any member's role, including promoting a Member to Admin or demoting an Admin back to Member. Contact support to request a role change until the self-serve interface is available.

## Removing a member

Contact Motorlytics support to remove a member from your team. Removed members immediately lose access to all team resources. Their personal session data is unaffected.

## Leaving a team as a member

Any member can leave a team themselves via **Settings → Account → Team Memberships → Leave**. Owners cannot leave if they are the only Owner. Transfer ownership first (contact support).
