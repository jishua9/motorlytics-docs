Member management is a **Team tier** feature. You must have the **Owner** or **Admin** role within a team to invite or manage other members.

## Team roles

Motorlytics has three team roles that control what each person can do within the team.

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full control over the team. Can invite and remove members, change roles, manage team settings, add and remove team cars, and create or delete shared resources. There must always be at least one Owner. |
| **Admin** | Can invite members and manage shared resources (cars, metrics, templates). Cannot change the Owner's role or delete the team. |
| **Member** | Can access all shared team resources — cars, templates, and metrics — but cannot invite others or change team settings. |

## Inviting a driver

Invitations link an existing Motorlytics account to your team. The driver must already have a Motorlytics account before they can be added.

To invite a driver (Owner or Admin only):

1. Navigate to your team's management page.
2. Select **Invite Member** (exact label may vary — if you cannot find it, contact the team Owner who set up the subscription).
3. Enter the driver's registered email address.
4. Choose the role to assign: **Admin** or **Member**.
5. Send the invitation.

The driver will see your team listed under **Settings → Account → Team Memberships** once they accept.

## Changing a member's role

Owners can change any member's role, including promoting a Member to Admin or demoting an Admin back to Member:

1. Open your team's member list.
2. Find the member whose role you want to change.
3. Select a new role from the role selector next to their name.

Admins can change Member roles but cannot change another Admin's role or the Owner's role.

## Removing a member

1. Open your team's member list.
2. Click **Remove** (or equivalent) next to the member.
3. Confirm the action.

Removed members immediately lose access to all team resources, including team cars, metrics, and templates. Their personal session data is unaffected.

## Leaving a team as a member

Any member can leave a team themselves via **Settings → Account → Team Memberships → Leave**. Owners cannot leave if they are the only Owner — transfer ownership first.
