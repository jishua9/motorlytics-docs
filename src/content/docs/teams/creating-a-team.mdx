## What is a team?

A Motorlytics team will be a shared workspace for a racing outfit. Once available, a team will have its own cars, custom setup metrics, and setup templates that every member can access. Each member will retain their own personal account and session data.

## Planned team capabilities

- Create a team with a name, description, logo, and website
- Invite drivers by email and assign them a role (Owner, Admin, or Member)
- Share cars across all team members from a single vehicle record
- Share custom setup metrics and templates so all drivers use consistent naming and units
- Owner and Admin roles can manage resources; Members can use them

## Viewing your current team memberships

If you have been added to a team by the Motorlytics support team, your memberships appear in **Settings → Account → Team Memberships**. Each entry shows the team name and logo, your role, and the date you joined.

## Leaving a team

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Team Memberships**.
2. Find the team you want to leave and click **Leave**.
3. Confirm in the dialog that appears.

:::caution
If you are the only **Owner**, you cannot leave the team until you transfer ownership to another member. Leaving removes your access to all team resources immediately.
:::
