Teams are a **Team tier** feature. You must be subscribed to the Team plan before you can create or manage a team.

## What is a team?

A Motorlytics team is a shared workspace for a racing outfit. Once created, the team has its own cars, custom setup metrics, and setup templates that every member can access. Each member retains their own personal account and session data.

## Creating a team

:::note[No self-serve team creation UI yet]
Team features are unlocked at the data level when you subscribe to the Team plan, but there is not yet a dedicated self-serve team-creation flow in the app. To have a team set up for your account, contact Motorlytics support after subscribing to the Team plan.
:::

Once your team has been created, it will appear under **Settings → Account → Team Memberships**. To update your team's profile details (name, description, logo, website), contact support or use the team settings controls available to the team **Owner**. The team stores the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Name** | Your team's display name |
| **Description** | A short bio or description for your team |
| **Logo** | A logo image URL for the team |
| **Website** | Your team's website URL |

## Viewing your team memberships

All teams you belong to appear in **Settings → Account → Team Memberships**. Each entry shows:

- The team name and logo
- Your role (Owner, Admin, or Member)
- The date you joined

## Leaving a team

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Team Memberships**.
2. Find the team you want to leave and click **Leave**.
3. Confirm in the dialog that appears.

:::caution
If you are the only **Owner**, you cannot leave the team until you transfer ownership to another member. Leaving removes your access to all team resources immediately.
:::
