Lap comparison is included with your Motorlytics subscription. You need at least two complete laps in the session for the delta chart to display.

## Opening the Lap Delta tab

1. Open a session with telemetry imported.
2. Select the **Telemetry** tab, then select **Lap Delta** from the sub-tab bar.

## Selecting laps

The **lap selector panel** floats over the left edge of the chart area. It lists all laps in the session with their lap times, best-lap highlighting, and the current selections.

- **Reference lap**: the baseline. All delta values are calculated relative to this lap. Defaults to the best (fastest) lap in the session.
- **Comparison laps**: one or more laps to compare against the reference. Select additional laps from the panel.

Each comparison lap is assigned a colour (blue, red, green, amber, purple) that is used consistently across all charts on this tab.

## Delta chart

The upper chart is headed **Delta (seconds) vs Lap X** where X is the reference lap number.

- The **vertical axis** shows time gain/loss in seconds, formatted as `+0.3s` / `−0.3s`.
- Positive values (above the zero line) mean the comparison lap is **slower** at that point in the lap.
- Negative values (below the zero line) mean the comparison lap is **faster** at that point.
- The **end-of-line labels** show the total delta for each comparison lap at the finish line (e.g. `Lap 5: +0.32s`).

The horizontal axis is lap distance (normalized). A dashed zero reference line sits at the centre.

## Speed chart

Below the delta chart is a **Speed (km/h)** chart showing the speed traces for all selected laps. The reference lap is drawn as a **dashed grey line**; comparison laps are drawn as solid coloured lines. This lets you see exactly where speed differences arise: earlier braking, slower apex speeds, or slower acceleration out of a corner.

## Zooming

Both charts share the same zoom state and zoom together:

- **Scroll wheel**: zoom in or out, centred on the mouse cursor position. A "Scroll to zoom" hint appears until the first scroll.
- **Click and drag**: draw a selection rectangle to zoom into that distance range.
- Click **Reset Zoom** (rotate-left icon) to return to the full lap view.

## Corner-by-Corner Analysis

When the event has a track layout with corners defined, a **Corner-by-Corner Analysis** table appears below the speed chart. It shows Entry, Min (apex), and Exit speeds plus the time spent in each corner, with delta indicators against the reference lap.

See [Corner Analysis](/telemetry/corner-analysis/) for a full explanation of the table.

## Charts tab: multi-channel comparison

For deeper channel comparison (throttle, brake, steering, RPM, G-force alongside speed), use the **Charts** tab instead:

1. Select up to three channels using the pills at the top of the tab.
2. Choose **Overlay** to draw all channels on one synchronized chart, or **Stacked** for separate panels per channel.
3. Use the Floating Lap Selector on the right to add or remove laps.

Channel selections and view mode (Overlay/Stacked) are saved per browser and persist across sessions.
