Session Health is available on **Pro and Team** plans and requires engine/health channels in the uploaded file. The view is only shown when at least one health channel is present — if your file contains GPS and speed data only, the Analysis tab shows "No engine data available for session health analysis".

## Accessing the Session Health view

1. Open a session with telemetry imported.
2. Select the **Telemetry** tab, then select the **Analysis** sub-tab.

The page is headed **Session Health Summary** and opens with an overall status banner.

## Overall status banner

The banner in the top-right of the section shows one of two states:

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **All parameters normal** (green) | Every channel with data is within its normal operating range |
| **X parameters outside range** (amber) | One or more channels exceeded a warning threshold during the session |

The count reflects all laps combined — a single spike in any channel during any lap is enough to trigger the warning.

## Engine RPM card

Shows **Peak RPM** (the highest single RPM value recorded) and the average. Below the headline figure, a bar chart shows **time-in-band distribution** across four bands:

| Band | Colour |
|------|--------|
| < 3,000 rpm | Green |
| 3,000 – 5,000 rpm | Blue |
| 5,000 – 6,500 rpm | Amber |
| > 6,500 rpm | Red |

Each bar is labelled with the percentage of total time spent in that band.

## Sensor gauge cards

Each engine health sensor that has data gets its own card showing **Min**, **Max**, and **Avg** values for the session, plus a **warning badge** if any threshold was exceeded.

### Oil Temperature

| Condition | Threshold | Badge |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Overheating | Max > **100 °C** | HIGH |
| Too cold | Min < **50 °C** | LOW |

### Oil Pressure

| Condition | Threshold | Badge |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Low pressure | Min < **1.0 bar** | LOW |

### Water Temperature

_(Labelled "Water Temperature" in the UI — also referred to as coolant temp.)_

| Condition | Threshold | Badge |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Overheating | Max > **95 °C** | HIGH |
| Too cold | Min < **60 °C** | LOW |

### Lambda / AFR

| Condition | Threshold | Badge |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Rich mixture | Avg < **0.9 λ** | RICH |
| Lean mixture | Avg > **1.1 λ** | LEAN |

The target lambda is **1.0** with a tolerance of **±0.1**. Note that this is an overall session average — brief off-throttle lean spikes are normal and will not trigger the warning by themselves.

### Battery Voltage

| Condition | Threshold | Badge |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Overcharging | Max > **15.0 V** | HIGH |
| Low voltage | Min < **11.5 V** | LOW |

### Internal Voltage

| Condition | Threshold | Badge |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Low voltage | Min < **3.0 V** | LOW |

### Logger Temperature

| Condition | Threshold | Badge |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Overheating | Max > **60 °C** | HIGH |

## Adaptive display

Gauge cards only appear for channels that have data. A session recorded with a basic GPS/speed logger will show no health cards at all. A full wiring loom recording oil temp, oil pressure, coolant, and lambda will show four sensor cards plus the RPM card.
